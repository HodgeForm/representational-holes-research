# From Search Failure to Language Limit

**Private research release candidate — NOT VERIFIED OR PUBLISHED**

Author: Derek Earnhart

## Research question
Can a frozen measurement language make distinct dynamics observationally indistinguishable, and can a targeted measurement extension resolve the ambiguity?

## Reported flagship experiment
Earlier research records report that a restricted translation-invariant measurement language did not distinguish elementary cellular automata Rules 3 and 17 across 2,032 enumerated programs. A separately specified parity-sensitive extension reportedly distinguished the rules on 217 of 256 tested states.

**These numerical results have not been independently reproduced for this release.** The language-wide claim depends on checking the precise grammar and symmetry argument, not merely the finite enumeration.
